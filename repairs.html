Before Your Repair

A Few Things to Keep in Mind

A little preparation can make the repair process easier and help protect your important information.

  • Back up important data where possible.
  • Bring the charger or relevant accessories if needed.
  • Tell us what happened and when the problem started.
  • If the device does not power on, bring it as it is.
  • For liquid damage, avoid repeatedly powering the device on before assessment.
