Before Your Repair

Before You Bring Your Device

A few simple steps can make the repair process easier and help protect your important information.

Back Up Your Data

Where possible, back up important photos, documents, contacts and other personal information.

Bring Relevant Accessories

If necessary, bring your charger, adapter or other accessories related to the problem.

Explain The Problem

Tell us what happened and what symptoms you noticed so we can understand the issue better.

Don't Worry If It Won't Power On

A device that does not turn on can still be assessed to determine the possible cause.
