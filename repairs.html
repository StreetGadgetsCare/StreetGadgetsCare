Before You Bring Your Device

A Few Helpful Things

A little preparation can make the repair process easier.

Back Up Your Data

Where possible, back up important files before submitting your device for repair.

Bring Relevant Accessories

If the issue involves charging or power, bring the charger or relevant accessory where possible.

Explain The Problem

Tell us what happened and what symptoms you have noticed on the device.

Don't Worry If It Won't Power On

If your device does not power on, bring it as it is so we can assess the problem.
