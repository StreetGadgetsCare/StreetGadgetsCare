Our Story

We Take Care of Your Technology

Street Gadgets Care is built around a simple idea: technology should make life easier, not become a constant source of frustration.

Based in Nairobi, we provide practical technology services for phones, laptops and other everyday devices. Our work includes repairs, device care, upgrades, accessories, gadgets and digital services.

We also serve customers beyond Nairobi. Customers across Kenya can reach us for our services and, where applicable, send their devices to us for repair and have them shipped back safely.
