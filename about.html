About Street Gadgets Care

Technology with care.

Street Gadgets Care is a Nairobi-based technology and creative support brand helping people and businesses solve everyday technology problems, find useful devices and present themselves professionally.

Who We Are

More than fixing devices.

Street Gadgets Care was built around a simple idea: technology should make life and work easier — not create unnecessary problems.

We work with individuals, students, professionals and businesses who need practical help with their devices, technology, creative work or digital needs.

Our foundation is technical, but our work goes beyond repairs. We bring technology, products, creative services and practical business support together.

Our Focus

Helping you solve the problem in front of you.

Whether it is a laptop that has stopped working, a phone that needs repair, a device you want to buy, or a business document that needs to look professional, we focus on understanding what you need and finding a practical way forward.

Our Story

We take care of the technology you depend on.

Technology has become part of almost everything we do. We use our phones to communicate, laptops to work, devices to learn and digital tools to run businesses.

When those things stop working or become difficult to manage, you need someone who can understand the problem and help you move forward.
