Who We Are

More than fixing devices.

Street Gadgets Care was built around a simple idea: technology should make life easier, not become another problem to deal with.

We work with phones, laptops, accessories and other technology while also helping individuals and businesses with creative design and practical digital support.

Our main focus is technology. From a device that has stopped working to a laptop that needs an upgrade, we approach every job with attention to detail and a focus on finding a practical solution.

Our Story
