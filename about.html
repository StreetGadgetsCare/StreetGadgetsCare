Who We Are

More than repairs. A partner in technology.

Street Gadgets Care is a Nairobi-based technology business providing phone and laptop repair, device sales, accessories, spare parts and creative design services.

We understand how important technology is in everyday life. From communication and work to learning and business, devices play an important role in keeping people connected and productive.

Our aim is to provide practical solutions that help customers make informed decisions, maintain their devices and access the technology services they need.

We bring device care, technology products and creative services together in one place.
Our Story

Built around solving everyday technology needs.

Street Gadgets Care was built around a simple idea: people need dependable support when it comes to their technology.

A device problem can interrupt work, studies, communication and business. Finding the right product, repair or technical support should not make that experience more difficult.

That is why we bring together repair services, technology products, accessories, spare parts and creative design — giving customers a place to seek practical support for different needs.
