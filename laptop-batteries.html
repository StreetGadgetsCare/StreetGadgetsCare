Original vs Replacement Laptop Batteries

We offer both original and replacement battery options because customers have different budgets and preferences. The right choice depends on your laptop model, battery availability and what you are looking for in a replacement.

  • Replacement Battery: A more affordable option for customers who want to restore their laptop's battery performance while keeping the replacement cost lower.
  • Original Battery: An original battery option for customers who prefer an original part designed for their compatible laptop model, where available.
  • Why are the prices different? Original and replacement batteries can differ in sourcing, battery type, availability and model-specific requirements. This is why prices vary between the two options.
  • Always confirm your model: Laptop batteries are model-specific. Before purchasing, provide your laptop's exact model so the correct battery can be identified.
