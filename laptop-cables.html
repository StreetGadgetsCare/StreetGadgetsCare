Before You Buy

Make sure the cable matches your setup.

Laptop power cables connect your wall power source to the laptop charger. The cable must be suitable for the charger and plug configuration you are using.

Check the Pin

Check whether your laptop charger uses a 2-pin or 3-pin power cable before ordering.

Check the Charger

If you're replacing an existing cable, compare it with your current charger before choosing a replacement.
