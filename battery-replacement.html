Choose the Right Battery for Your Device

Battery replacement is an important part of keeping your phone or laptop reliable and ready for everyday use. At Street Gadgets Care, we provide different battery options to suit different devices, budgets and customer preferences.

  • Phone Batteries: Replacement and original battery options are available for selected phone brands and models.
  • Laptop Batteries: Original and replacement options are available for various laptop brands and models.
  • Exact model matters: Batteries are not universal. Always provide your exact device model so the correct battery can be identified.
  • Professional installation: We can help you identify the suitable battery and professionally install it for your device.
